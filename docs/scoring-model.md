# Scoring model

Archic Benchmark measures quality relative to purpose. A premium restaurant, luxury car rental company, real-estate platform and SaaS product use different category weights and business expectations.

The eleven dimensions are Business fit, Visual design, UX and architecture, Conversion, Mobile, Performance, SEO and GEO, Content and copy, Accessibility, Security and trust, and Robustness.

A weighted average produces the raw score. Quality gates can then cap the final score when a material functional, mobile, performance, trust or availability problem exists. This prevents strong visual work from hiding a broken commercial journey.

The tiers are: below 60 Needs work; 60–69 Acceptable; 70–79 Good; 80–89 Premium; 90–94 Exceptional; and 95–100 Reference Quality. The top tier is intentionally difficult to earn.

Automated browser evidence is authoritative for measurable facts. The optional visual AI reviewer contributes to qualitative areas such as perceived value, hierarchy, business fit and conversion craft, but it does not replace technical measurements.

Priorities are ranked using business impact, severity, niche-specific category weight and estimated effort. Percentiles are withheld until a niche has a meaningful reference sample.
