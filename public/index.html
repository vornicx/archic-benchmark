QUALITY SYSTEM

Benchmark standards

The rules that keep a high score difficult to earn.

01

Relative to purpose

A restaurant, luxury rental company and SaaS product cannot share the same definition of excellence. Category weights and expectations change by niche.

02

Gates before polish

Critical functional, mobile or trust failures cap the final score. A visually beautiful site cannot hide a broken commercial journey.

03

Objective + visual

Browser measurements, markup checks and runtime failures are combined with a strict visual/business review. Neither layer is allowed to dominate blindly.

04

95 is exceptional

Reference Quality begins at 95. The system is intentionally calibrated so the portfolio does not drift toward meaningless 98/100 scores.
